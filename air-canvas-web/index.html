AirCanvas 🎨🖐️

Draw in the air with your index finger. Everything runs in your browser; no video leaves your device.

Allow camera access when asked.

Gestures

  • Index up, middle bent: draw
  • Index + middle up: pen up / select. Hold your fingertip on a button for ~0.6s to press it
  • Fist or open palm: idle

Keys

  • U undo   C clear   S save
  • + / - brush size

Tips

  • Light your hand from the front
  • Stay 40 to 80 cm away, palm facing the camera
